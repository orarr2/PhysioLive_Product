# Squat corpus

Evidence on the bodyweight squat used by PhysioLive when it detects a
squat rep. Covers four themes:

- **Depth targets.** Rehabilitation squat depth is progressed within
  the patient's pain-free range; parallel thigh (~90 deg knee flexion)
  is a common functional benchmark, not a mandatory floor.
- **Knee alignment (KOT).** Excessive forward translation of the knee
  past the toe increases patellofemoral compressive force. Cue: sit
  back, hips first, shin roughly vertical.
- **Torso lean.** A moderate forward torso lean is normal; over about
  45 deg from vertical shifts load from the quads to the lumbar spine.
- **Tempo.** Eccentric-heavy tempo (about 3 sec down, 1 to 2 sec up) is
  useful for tendon rehab; very fast reps limit form control.

Chunks in this folder are retrieved automatically for any exercise
tagged as a squat variant in `src/app/exercises/`.
