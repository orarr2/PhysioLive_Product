# Knee corpus

Cross-cutting knee evidence that applies to more than one exercise:

- **Dynamic knee valgus.** Medial knee tracking during squats, lunges
  or landings is a risk factor for ACL injury and patellofemoral pain.
- **Range of motion utilisation.** The ratio of used to available
  range; consistent under-utilisation slows strength adaptation.
- **Symmetry index.** Between-limb comparison used as a return-to-play
  criterion in late-stage rehabilitation.

Chunks in this folder are retrieved whenever the coach query is
tagged with `knee`.
