# General corpus

Cross-exercise material: measurement notes, warm-up guidance, pain
rules, and camera setup. These chunks are always in scope regardless
of the exercise the user is doing.

- **Rep counting.** Why we require a two-tick confirmation before
  switching state.
- **Camera view.** When to prefer front, side, or 45-degree oblique.
- **Warm-up.** WHO-style guidance to raise tissue temperature before
  loaded rehabilitation work.
- **Pain.** When mild discomfort is acceptable and when to stop.
