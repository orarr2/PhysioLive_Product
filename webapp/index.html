How it works

Three moving parts, all working together in your browser.

01

Camera + pose

Your webcam feeds MediaPipe Pose in the browser. 33 body landmarks and 21 finger joints per hand, at ~15 fps on any modern phone.

02

Rep counter + rules

A direction-agnostic state machine counts every rep. Deterministic form rules score depth, alignment, and torso lean at rep-close.

03

Coach feedback

For each verdict, the app pulls the most relevant chunks from a physiotherapy knowledge base and asks a language model to compose a single, cited sentence.
